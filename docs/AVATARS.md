# Your adventurer

Tap your avatar on Android, or Your adventurer on Windows. Style, Characters and Journey keep appearance, character selection and progression together. Names, Boy/Girl looks, hair, expressions, colours, HD/Pixel and animation preferences save immediately.

The personal avatar is customizable. Signature characters equip as complete fixed designs; switching back retains your personal appearance. Android 1.2.8 replaces the older class/rank gallery with the approved whole-character portraits and the curated collection below. An equipped retired class returns to the personal avatar without resetting XP, name, identity or colours.

## Motion

HD and Pixel display the same approved artwork. Idle animation and tap-to-cheer keep the character whole. Madara and Sukuna have in-card skills; Zoro's sword skill belongs to his mastery outfit. Use skill previews the selected attack. Automatic skills play only for earned characters and can be disabled separately. A locked skill preview cannot grant an unlock or XP.

During transfers, the sender is on the left and receiver on the right, with progress between them. Tap either avatar to animate its own frame. Both use this device's motion settings. Animation stops when disabled, when the app leaves the foreground, or when the system disables animator effects. Older peers show a placeholder when avatar metadata is unavailable.

## XP and saved data

Verified new content earns one XP per complete 256 KiB, capped at 50 per file. Smaller files share normally but earn no XP. Repeated or renamed copies, retries, cancelled or corrupt transfers and previews earn no additional reward. Existing XP and unlock thresholds remain unchanged.

Total XP for level L is 40n² + 3n³, where n = L - 1. Level 100 requires 3,302,937 XP. No currency, account, streak or sharing restriction is attached to progression. Features and transfer speed are available at every level.

Progress remains local to each installation. Android retains its SQLite receipt ledger; Windows retains its checksummed receipt ledger. Neither is reset by an ordinary signed update. Removing app data removes local progress. Cosmetic metadata never authorizes transfers, changes another device's progress or replaces QR approval and file verification.
